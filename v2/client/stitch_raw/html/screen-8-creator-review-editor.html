lightbulb Explanation & Pedagogical Rationale

According to the Born rule, the probability of obtaining outcome |i⟩ upon measurement is given by P(i) = |⟨i|ψ⟩|². For the symmetric superposition state (|0⟩ + |1⟩)/√2, both coefficients squared equal (1/√2)² = 1/2, creating an unbiased 50/50 measurement collapse into one of the discrete basis states.
