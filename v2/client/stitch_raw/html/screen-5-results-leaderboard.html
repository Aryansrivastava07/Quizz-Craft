Applying a Hadamard gate \( H \) followed by a Pauli-Z gate \( Z \) on the basis state \( |0\rangle \) yields which pure state?

A \( |-\rangle = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(|0\rangle - |1\rangle) \)
check_circle Your Answer • Correct
B \( |+\rangle = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(|0\rangle + |1\rangle) \)
C \( |1\rangle \)
D \( \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(|0\rangle - i|1\rangle) \)
menu_book Pedagogical Rationale & Proof
expand_more

First, the Hadamard operator puts \(|0\rangle\) into the equal superposition state: \(H|0\rangle = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}(|0\rangle + |1\rangle) = |+\rangle\).

Next, the Pauli-Z gate acts as a phase-flip operator: \(Z|0\rangle = |0\rangle\) and \(Z|1\rangle = -|1\rangle\). Therefore:

\( Z(H|0\rangle) = Z\left(\frac{|0\rangle + |1\rangle}{\sqrt{2}}\right) = \frac{|0\rangle - |1\rangle}{\sqrt{2}} = |-\rangle \)

In Shor's Algorithm for integer factorization of \( N = pq \), what is the primary computational bottleneck handled on the classical coprocessor rather than the quantum processing unit?

A Computing the Quantum Fourier Transform (QFT)
cancel Your Selection
B Continued fraction expansion & GCD extraction
check_circle Correct Solution
C Generating initial superposition over \( 2^n \) states
D Unitary modular exponentiation \( a^x \pmod N \)
psychology Diagnostic Breakdown & Why You Slipped:

The Quantum Fourier Transform (QFT) is executed completely on the quantum register in \( \mathcal{O}(n^2) \) gates to reveal the period phase \( s/r \). Once the measurement is collapsed, the resulting phase estimate is passed to the classical processor.

Formula: \( \frac{s}{r} \approx \frac{y}{2^n} \implies \gcd(a^{r/2} \pm 1, N) \) via classical Euclidean Algorithm in polynomial time \( \mathcal{O}(\log^3 N) \).

Key takeaway: Quantum devices solve only the non-polynomial Period-Finding subroutine; classic arithmetic handles continued fractions.
